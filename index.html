How the score works

Purity v2

Ingredients are classified as wholesome, neutral, questionable, or noxious, then weighted by ingredient-list position. Ingredients explicitly listed at 2% or less receive a trace-weight reduction.

P = 100 × Σ(qᵢwᵢ) / Σwᵢ

Nutritional Balance

Nutrition is normalized to 100 kcal and scored against the 50/25/25 macro target, 5%-DV micronutrient target, 1.4 g fiber target, and limit nutrients.

B = .35M + .35A + .10F + .20L

Health Index

The harmonic mean makes the weaker of Purity and Balance matter. One dimension cannot fully compensate for a poor score in the other.

Index = 2PB / (P + B)
